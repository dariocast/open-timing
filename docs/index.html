Features

Crafted for privacy, speed, and precision.

Everything you need to understand how you spend your time on macOS, with zero bloat.

🔒

100% Offline-First

All logs stay in your local SQLite database. No network requests, zero telemetry, and complete independence from cloud servers.

⚡

Native Swift & Low Energy

Engineered directly in Swift and SwiftUI. OpenTiming uses native macOS workspace events and consumes near-zero CPU and battery.

🔍

Window & URL Tracking

Inspects active window titles, documents, and active browser tabs across Safari, Google Chrome, Arc, Brave, and Edge.

🎯

Smart Rules & Regex

Categorize your time automatically. Create rules matching app names, titles, or web domains with prioritized conditions and retroactive tagging.

⏱️

24h Interactive Timeline

A visual chronological ribbon shows exactly what you did minute-by-minute. Search by keyword or re-tag activities on the fly.

📥

Instant CSV & JSON Export

Your data belongs to you. Export your logs anytime to clean CSV spreadsheets or JSON for custom analysis and reporting.

Quick Start

Up and running in 30 seconds.

01

Download or Clone

Download the pre-compiled OpenTiming.app from GitHub Releases or clone the repository locally.

02

Grant Accessibility

Allow Accessibility permissions in System Settings so OpenTiming can read window titles and browser tabs.

03

Focus on Your Work

OpenTiming automatically tracks, categorizes, and logs your workflow in the background. No manual timers needed.
