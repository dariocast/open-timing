Design Philosophy

The case for offline-first activity tracking.

Most modern time tracking tools are built on a software-as-a-service model: they continuously stream every window title you open, every document you edit, and every URL you browse to remote servers.

We believe your work telemetry is confidential. Where you spend your hours, the code you write, and the documents you read should not require an account, an internet connection, or a monthly fee.

OpenTiming is engineered with a strict principle: 100% of data is stored and processed locally in SQLite. No network requests. Zero telemetry.

Capabilities

Natively integrated with macOS.

01

Native Accessibility Tracking

Monitors frontmost apps, window titles, and document paths with minimal CPU footprint using macOS AXUIElement and NSWorkspace APIs.

02

Browser Tab & URL Inspection

Extracts active domains and full URLs across Safari, Google Chrome, Arc, Brave, and Edge to separate productive research from leisure.

03

Idle & Inactivity Filtering

Uses hardware event timers (CGEventSource) to detect when you step away from your Mac, preventing idle hours from skewing your metrics.

04

Smart Rules & Regex Engine

Automatically categorize time by app bundle ID, title pattern, or web domain. Retroactively apply new rules across your entire database in one click.

05

24-Hour Interactive Timeline

A continuous visual ribbon displays your work rhythm throughout the day. Search events by keyword or manually re-assign categories.

06

Direct Data Export

Your logs belong to you. Export your entire timeline or custom ranges to standard CSV spreadsheets (Excel/Numbers) or raw JSON.

Get Started

Build from source or run instantly.

OpenTiming requires macOS 14.0 (Sonoma) or newer on Apple Silicon or Intel.

Step 1

Clone the repository and run with Swift Package Manager:

git clone https://github.com/dariocast/open-timing.git
cd open-timing
swift run OpenTiming
Step 2

Or build a release .app bundle:

./scripts/build_app.sh
open ./build/OpenTiming.app
Step 3

When prompted, grant Accessibility permissions in System Settings > Privacy & Security to enable window and browser tab detection.
